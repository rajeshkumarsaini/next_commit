# Next Commit

A calm, 4-minute career check-in for new computer science graduates navigating the AI era.

**Live site:** https://rajeshkumarsaini.github.io/next_commit/

## What it does

- **15 everyday questions** about travel, money, work-life balance, job security, problem solving and new ideas, written for new grads with no jargon.
- **14 future-ready careers in 4 worlds:** Build the Tech (tech services), Tech + an Industry (industry specialist), People & Strategy (consulting), and Start Your Own (entrepreneurship).
- **Results** show the top 3 careers, best-fit world, a priorities chart, and all 14 careers ranked.
- **For each career:** starter job titles, skills to build, 3 example employers with HQ city and links to their internship / new-grad programs, US pay ranges (starting and after 1–3 years, with sources), and what 1–3 years at those firms leads to.
- **A 9-step application plan** with a progress checklist, saved-company shortlist, referral message template and interview prep for that career.
- **Anxiety-aware design:** a vibe check, a 30-second breathing break, and a "two-way door" reminder that first jobs aren't forever.

No sign-up and no server. Everything runs in one HTML file, and answers stay in the visitor's own browser.

## Updating

Edit `index.html` and push to `main`. GitHub Pages republishes automatically within a minute or two.

Company links, pay ranges and program details change through the year. Re-check them before each recruiting season.
